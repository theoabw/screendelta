## The two equivalent mutants

An equivalent mutant is a change that no test can distinguish because it changes nothing observable. Both are
recorded here rather than left to look like a gap, and both were confirmed by reasoning after the run:

**The pixel word size.** The comparison skips a row in words of eight bytes once it knows the row changed, and
it only skips a word when the two words are equal. A smaller word therefore skips less and finds the same
pixels; the rule affects how much work the comparison does and never what it reports.

**The last key of the region order.** Making the height comparison non-strict can only matter for two regions
identical in position, identity and height, where the order is arbitrary whichever way the comparison goes. The
keys before it settle every case that a consumer can observe.

## The third survivor, and why no test was added for it

The size gate's lower bound is equivalent in a subtler way, and the first attempt at a test for it was wrong.
An area a tenth the size of an element, sharing a corner, is matched to that element, and it should be: it is
inside the element, and the containment rule exists to match a part of a larger whole. Relaxing the size gate
from half to a twentieth changes no identity for that case. What it does change is which of the two matching
explanations answers, and therefore the confidence: containment reports one, because the area is certainly part
of an element the engine is tracking, while overlap reports the overlap, because it rests on a partial match.
The test asserts the confidence, which is the observable difference, and the false-premise test was removed with
its reason recorded in the source rather than quietly deleted.
